Trading ledger

Fills are simulated at the next available session open, checked against risk limits, with 10 basis points of adverse slippage per side. No intraday fill or queue modelling. Slippage is already reflected in prices; it is not deducted twice.

System & setup

Your part of the trial

  1. Generate an Upstox access token and save it using the local token setup. Keep passwords and tokens out of chat.
  2. Start the Upstox paper app. Keep this computer awake and connected around market close.
  3. Replace the token when Upstox requires reauthorization. The app reloads the token automatically.
  4. Review warnings and verify applicable charges and corporate actions. Review the exported report at the end.

No real capital, paid AI model, or cloud server is needed for this local prototype.

What the trial can establish

Data availability, consistent decisions, restart recovery, ledger accuracy, and correct handling of failures.

What remains unproven

Profitability, actual fill quality, corporate-action accounting, and performance across market conditions. This fixed universe is for operational testing, not a survivorship-free historical study.

Costs use a configurable Upstox approximation. Operating expense defaults to ₹0 for a local trial; add your actual recurring cost before a new trial. Results exclude personal income tax.
